# Section 3.3 – Proof by Contrapositive

*Exercise text copied from photos of the textbook's Section 3.3 Exercises. Homework: 16, 18, 21.*

## 16

Let $x \in \mathbb{Z}$. Prove that if $7x + 5$ is odd, then $x$ is even.

## 18

Let $x \in \mathbb{Z}$. Prove that $5x - 11$ is even if and only if $x$ is odd.

## 21

Let $n \in \mathbb{Z}$. Prove that $(n + 1)^2 - 1$ is even if and only if $n$ is even.
