# Section 3.4 – Proof by Cases

*Exercise text copied from photos of the textbook's Section 3.4 Exercises. Homework: 26, 28, 59.*

## 26

Prove that if $n \in \mathbb{Z}$, then $n^2 - 3n + 9$ is odd.

## 28

Let $x, y \in \mathbb{Z}$. Prove that if $xy$ is odd, then $x$ and $y$ are odd.


## 59

*From the Additional Exercises for Chapter 3.*

Prove for every two distinct real numbers $a$ and $b$ that either $\frac{a + b}{2} > a$ or $\frac{a + b}{2} > b$.
